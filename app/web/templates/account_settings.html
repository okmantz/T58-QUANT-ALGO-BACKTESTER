Saved runs & account

Everything is filed under your license key. Your reports, pipeline and validation runs, strategy graveyard, journal and saved strategies, plus your profile (name, username, picture, lock password), achievements, background, theme and colour, and your API keys, are copied to a folder an uninstall doesn't touch — and put back automatically the moment you sign in again after a reinstall. API keys and broker passwords are stored encrypted with your license key, never as plain files, and only fill fields that are still empty. The license key itself is never copied. Works with the master key too.
