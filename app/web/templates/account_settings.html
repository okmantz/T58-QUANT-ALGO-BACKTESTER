Saved runs

Your reports, strategy graveyard, journal and saved strategies are copied to a folder that an uninstall doesn't touch, and are put back automatically the next time the app starts. Sign in with the same email and license key after a reinstall and you pick up where you left off — no need to re-run the pipeline. API keys, passwords and your license key are never copied.
