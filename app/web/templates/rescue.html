Rescue a weak strategy

The normal optimizer only moves the numbers a strategy already has. Rescue changes its structure: which side it trades, its stops and targets, how trades end, and which hours it trades. It chooses on the older 70% of the data, then judges the winner on the newest 30% that no variant ever saw. Every variant tried is counted as a trial.
