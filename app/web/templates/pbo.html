PBO

Probability of Backtest Overfitting: given a POOL of candidates (a Search Lab batch, a hand-picked shortlist, or several perturbed variants of one idea), ranks every candidate by IN-SAMPLE performance on each combinatorial path and checks where that same "winner" actually ranked OUT-OF-sample. A high PBO means picking the best-looking backtest out of several tried candidates is more likely to be luck than skill.
