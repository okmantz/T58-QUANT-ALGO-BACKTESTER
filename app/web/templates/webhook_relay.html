TradingView webhook relay

Run your Pine Script strategy on TradingView and let its alerts drive a forward test here. Every alert moves a T58 paper account (the demo account: real-time signals, simulated fills, full P&L stats), and can optionally mirror to a demo/practice broker account you have saved under Deploy. Live accounts are refused.

How it fits together

  1. Pine strategy on TradingView places entries/exits on the chart as usual.
  2. A TradingView alert on that strategy sends a webhook to the URL below, carrying the order details.
  3. T58 turns it into a position change on the paper account, and (if you chose one) on your demo broker account.

TradingView's own Paper Trading cannot be driven by webhooks, so T58's paper account plays that role: it keeps the position, fills, P&L and win rate for the forward test.

1 · Public address TradingView can reach {% if url_ready %}ready{% else %}needed{% endif %}

Why this step exists: TradingView's servers call your webhook from the internet, and they only accept https on port 443 (or http on port 80). This app runs on port {{ local_port }} on your own computer, so an address like http://127.0.0.1:{{ local_port }} (or a Tailscale 100.x.x.x:{{ local_port }} one) is rejected with "Only port 80 is allowed for HTTP". A tunnel fixes that: it gives you a public https address with no port that forwards to this app.

Easiest: let T58 open the tunnel for you

Nothing to install: this uses the free Cloudflare quick tunnel if you have it, otherwise the ssh program already built into Windows 10+, macOS and Linux (through localhost.run). No account. Click once and the address below fills itself in.

Or do it by hand with any tunnel you already use:

  1. Pick one tunnel and run its command in a terminal (leave it running):
    ngrok (easiest)
    ngrok http {{ local_port }}
    Cloudflare Tunnel
    cloudflared tunnel --url http://localhost:{{ local_port }}
    Tailscale Funnel
    tailscale funnel {{ local_port }}
  2. Copy the https:// address it prints (no :{{ local_port }} on the end), paste it below, and click Save & test.

3 · Set up the alert in TradingView

  1. Add your Pine strategy to a chart (the chart's symbol and timeframe are what the alert will trade).
  2. Click Alerts → Create alert. Condition: your strategy, with "Order fills only".
  3. Open the Notifications tab, tick Webhook URL and paste the URL from your relay. It starts with https:// and has no port number. If TradingView shows "Only port 80 is allowed", the URL has a port like :{{ local_port }} in it: redo step 1.
  4. In the Message box, replace the text with the alert message from your relay. TradingView fills the {{ '{{' }}placeholders{{ '}}' }} when the alert fires.
  5. Save. Use Test it from here first to check the whole chain before you wait for a real signal.

TradingView requires a paid plan with webhooks and two-factor sign-in enabled on your account. Entries and exits both need an alert; "Order fills only" on a strategy alert covers both. The relay reads the strategy's resulting position, so duplicate or reordered alerts can't stack extra size.

The paper account fills at the price in the alert and applies the instrument's standard round-turn commission but no slippage, so expect real fills to be a little worse.
