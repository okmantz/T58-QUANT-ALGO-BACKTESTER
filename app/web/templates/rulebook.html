v2 designs a strategy backwards from the account's rules and pay structure, then spends fees as a capped sequential test. Every engine in this app (create, test, optimize, validate, deploy) is held to the same eight gates. A gate that fails files the idea with the failing number; it is not tuned and re-run.

G3: the observed edge you need

Observed net edge (R) that leaves a planning edge E_plan of +0.05R after N looks (σR = 1.46, a 2:1 payoff). Each extra look costs roughly 100–200 trades, which is why looks are capped and trades are bought with breadth.
