💡 What this section is for

Optimize takes a strategy that already exists (built in Create) and searches for parameter values that perform better than whatever you started with -- entry/exit thresholds, indicator lengths, stop/target distances, and similar numeric knobs -- rather than inventing new trading logic from scratch. Six methods below trade off speed, how broadly they search, and how much setup they need from you; if you're not sure which to reach for, Full Pipeline is the recommended default: it runs search, refinement, and out-of-sample validation together in one pass. Whatever comes out still needs Validate afterward to check it isn't overfit.

Or pick a specific method

Fastest

Quick Optimize

Auto-tunes one strategy's own parameters toward pass/win-rate/payout targets. The lightest-weight option.
Broadest

Search Lab

Generates and filters hundreds of strategy variations across a large parameter space, then refines and validates the best ones.
Evolutionary

Evolution Lab (GA)

A genetic algorithm evolves parameter sets across generations rather than sampling a fixed grid.
Multi-goal

Multi-Objective

Optimizes profit, drawdown, and consistency together instead of collapsing them into one fitness number, and shows the trade-off frontier between them.
Focused

Iterative Refinement

Repeated rounds of local parameter search around a strategy you already like, rather than exploring broadly from scratch.
One dial, big effect

Risk Sweep

Holds your strategy fixed and tests risk-per-trade from 0.10% to 1.00% against the full prop-survival model to find which level actually maximizes odds of payout -- not just which looks best on one backtest.
