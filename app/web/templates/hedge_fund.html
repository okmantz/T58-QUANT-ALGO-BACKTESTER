2. Research desk (view generation)

Samples many resampled paths per asset and uses the mean as the view, the dispersion as the confidence fed to the portfolio desk -- the same architecture the source article uses Kronos for, without vendoring an unproven forecasting model. (Per-asset "use one of my saved strategies as its view" is available from the Python API; this form always uses the statistical bootstrap for now.)

3. Portfolio desk (Black-Litterman)

Confidence is the Omega dial: 1.0 trusts the research desk's own measured dispersion exactly; near 0 makes the optimizer ignore the views and fall back to equal-weight equilibrium.
