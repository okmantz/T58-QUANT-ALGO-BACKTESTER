2 Research desk how views are formed

Default (no strategy picked above): samples many resampled paths per asset and uses the mean as the view, the dispersion as the confidence fed to the portfolio desk -- the same architecture the source article uses Kronos for, without vendoring an unproven forecasting model. Assets with a Strategy Library pick above use that strategy's current signal and historical same-direction hit rate instead.

3 Portfolio desk how views become weights

Confidence is the Omega dial: 1.0 trusts the research desk's own measured dispersion exactly; near 0 makes the optimizer ignore the views and fall back to equal-weight equilibrium.
