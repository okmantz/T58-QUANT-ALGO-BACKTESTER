TradingView bridge

Every backtest report carries a short settings ID. Paste it into a T58 Pine script and the exact configuration the backtest ran with shows up on your chart: strategy, market, timeframe, risk per trade, daily loss limit, drawdown limit and account size.

1Copy the ID

Open any report and press Copy ID in its TradingView section.

2Add the script once

In TradingView's Pine editor, paste one of the two scripts below and add it to your chart.

3Paste the ID

Open the script's settings and paste the ID into its one input. Done.

Check an ID

Catches half-copied or edited IDs before you paste them into TradingView.

Indicator: shows the settings on the chart

A table in the corner of your chart listing every setting. Works on any chart; changes nothing about trading.

Strategy: also enforces the protections

Sizes each trade by the backtest's risk setting and stops trading when the max trades per day, daily loss limit or drawdown limit is hit. The entry signal is a placeholder (EMA cross): replace the two lines marked YOUR ENTRY LOGIC with your own.
