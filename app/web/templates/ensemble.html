Multi-strategy ensemble

The mirror case of Multi-Asset Portfolio: several DIFFERENT strategies combined on the SAME instrument, instead of one strategy across several instruments. Blend keeps every strategy trading independently at a correlation-adjusted share of the account's risk budget. Vote combines every strategy's signal into one entry, taken only once enough of them agree on direction (risk management inherited from the first leg).
