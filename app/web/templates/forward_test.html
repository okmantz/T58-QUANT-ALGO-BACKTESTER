🧭 What happens next

Forward-test clean → watch it in Live Market.
Missed a move? Replay plays it back bar by bar.
Real-money deployment lives in Deploy → Deploy Live — separately guarded and armed on purpose.

⚠ Watch-outs

Forward testing is not backtesting: latency, spread and the terminal's own disconnects are all real here. A clean week is the minimum before real money — not a single good day.
