What happens after you press Start

Fast, and strict about luck. A winner has to survive every step; near-misses are shown with the exact step they failed.

1 · Pick bar sizeIf trading costs would eat a small-bar strategy alive, it moves up to bigger bars automatically.
2 · Search18 strategy types, thousands of settings, stops sized in ATR so costs stay small compared with the risk taken.
3 · Fresh dataThe best ideas must also work on bars they never trained on.
4 · Nudge testNeighbouring settings must be profitable too. A real edge is a plateau, not a spike.
5 · Sealed testThe last 20% of data is read once, with double costs. This is the lie detector.
6 · Save & testSave a winner, then run it through Full Pipeline and Validate. A find is a lead, not a verdict.
