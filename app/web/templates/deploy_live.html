Access control (v7): these routes are disabled entirely unless the server was started with T58_ENABLE_WEB_LIVE_DEPLOY=1 and an app-lock password is set under Account Settings with this browser session unlocked. Enabling the flag without the password lock keeps every route refused. If this page shows a "disabled" error, that is the guard working -- do not expose this server's port beyond localhost unless you understand exactly who can reach it.

Enforced prop guardrails (every web-launched session)

News blackout ON (07:25–07:40, 12:55–13:10 server/broker time -- US releases/FOMC) · weekend holds BLOCKED (flattened Friday) · hedging BLOCKED · max 5 contracts/lots per single order · daily-loss limit halts new entries · max-drawdown halt armed -- a breach flattens ALL positions and stops the session · 3 consecutive poll failures also flatten and halt. Sizing units are converted to broker-native quantity (whole contracts on Tradovate, lots elsewhere) -- never passed through raw.

3. Copy trades to other accounts (optional)

The account picked in step 1 is the leader: it trades the strategy. Tick other accounts to make them followers. Every trade the leader places is copied to each follower and closed when the leader closes, sized on that account's own equity (or a multiple of the leader's exposure, or a fixed size) and guarded by that account's own drawdown, daily-loss and news rules. Leave a field blank to use the leader's value from step 4. Followers only copy trades the leader actually placed. If one follower breaches its drawdown or loses its connection it flattens and halts alone; the rest keep trading.
