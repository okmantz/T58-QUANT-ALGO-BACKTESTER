Where to send it

This is the address that receives the "job finished" email -- it doesn't need to match the SMTP account below.

Outgoing mail server (SMTP)

Any SMTP-capable mail account works, including a free Gmail/Outlook account -- for Gmail specifically, use an app password, not your normal login password. Nothing here is sent anywhere except directly to the SMTP host you enter -- it's stored locally on this computer only.

Leave this blank when re-saving other fields to keep the password you already have on file.
