What was added to the strategy builders

Indicators (Search Lab, Evolution Lab, Manual builder, Cross-instrument, Research Loop): gex_regime, gex_flip_distance, gex_call_wall_distance, gex_put_wall_distance, theta_clock, gamma_convexity, ema_stack_score, bull_flag_score, volume_expansion.

Families: GEX wall fade, GEX flip trend, GEX flip reclaim, negative-gamma breakout, positive-gamma pin fade, gamma convexity momentum, EMA stack + flag + volume, call/put wall breakout.

Honest limits: CBOE's free feed is delayed ~15 minutes and only describes today; ES maps to the SPX chain, NQ to NDX, GC to the GLD ETF. There is no free per-strike history, so older days use the proxy unless you saved or imported levels.
