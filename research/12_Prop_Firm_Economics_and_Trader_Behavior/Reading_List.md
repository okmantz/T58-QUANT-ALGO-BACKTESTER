# 12 - Prop-Firm Economics, Discipline and Trader Behaviour

Why this folder: passing an evaluation is a stopping problem with a trailing drawdown, a fee and human behaviour. These papers cover the maths of that problem and why traders stray from their rules.

Links were written from the authors' and publishers' public records and have NOT been opened or downloaded by the tool that built this list (its sandbox cannot reach arXiv, SSRN or publishers). arXiv and DOI links point straight at the paper; Google Scholar links are searches that lead to the free copy. Run `python scripts/check_paper_links.py` on your own machine to flag any dead link, and correct what it reports.

## Papers

1. Barber, Lee, Liu, Odean - "Do Retail Day Traders Lose Money? Evidence from Taiwan", Review of Finance 2014.
   - Role: Only about one percent of day traders earn reliably positive returns after costs; the honest baseline.
   - Links: https://scholar.google.com/scholar?q=Barber+Lee+Liu+Odean+Day+Trading+Taiwan

2. Chague, De-Losso, Giovannetti - "Day Trading for a Living?", SSRN 2020.
   - Role: Almost nobody earns more than the minimum wage after persisting for 300 days in Brazil.
   - Links: https://scholar.google.com/scholar?q=Chague+De-Losso+Giovannetti+Day+Trading+for+a+Living

3. Odean - "Do Investors Trade Too Much?", American Economic Review 1999.
   - Role: Overconfidence and excess trading cost investors; a case for fewer, rule-based trades.
   - Links: https://doi.org/10.1257/aer.89.5.1279

4. Odean - "Are Investors Reluctant to Realize Their Losses?", Journal of Finance 1998.
   - Role: The disposition effect: selling winners too early and holding losers.
   - Links: https://doi.org/10.1111/0022-1082.00072

5. Kahneman, Tversky - "Prospect Theory: An Analysis of Decision under Risk", Econometrica 1979.
   - Role: Why traders feel losses more than gains; the root of premature exits and revenge trades.
   - Links: https://doi.org/10.2307/1914185

6. Thaler, Johnson - "Gambling with the House Money and Trying to Break Even", Management Science 1990.
   - Role: Risk taking changes after wins and losses; relevant to daily-loss rules.
   - Links: https://doi.org/10.1287/mnsc.36.6.643

7. Coates, Herbert - "Endogenous Steroids and Financial Risk Taking on a London Trading Floor", PNAS 2008.
   - Role: Trader physiology and risk taking.
   - Links: https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.0704025105

8. Lo, Repin - "The Psychophysiology of Real-Time Financial Risk Processing", Journal of Cognitive Neuroscience 2002.
   - Role: Even experienced traders show strong physical stress reactions to volatility.
   - Links: https://doi.org/10.1162/089892902317361877

9. Shefrin, Statman - "The Disposition to Sell Winners Too Early and Ride Losers Too Long", Journal of Finance 1985.
   - Role: The original disposition-effect paper.
   - Links: https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1540-6261.1985.tb05002.x

10. Gneezy, Potters - "An Experiment on Risk Taking and Evaluation Periods", Quarterly Journal of Economics 1997.
   - Role: Checking results less often increases risk taking; a reason to review on a schedule.
   - Links: https://doi.org/10.1162/003355397555217

11. Feller - "An Introduction to Probability Theory and Its Applications, Vol. 1 (gambler's ruin chapter)", Wiley 1968.
   - Role: The standard treatment of ruin probabilities, the maths behind pass/bust rates.
   - Links: https://scholar.google.com/scholar?q=Feller+gambler%27s+ruin+random+walk+probability+theory

12. Lo - "Where Do Alphas Come From? A New Measure of the Value of Active Investment Management", Journal of Investment Management 2008.
   - Role: Separate skill from luck in a track record.
   - Links: https://scholar.google.com/scholar?q=Lo+Where+Do+Alphas+Come+From

13. Fama, French - "Luck versus Skill in the Cross-Section of Mutual Fund Returns", Journal of Finance 2010.
   - Role: A bootstrap method for deciding whether a track record exceeds luck.
   - Links: https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1540-6261.2010.01606.x

14. Kosowski, Timmermann, Wermers, White - "Can Mutual Fund Stars Really Pick Stocks?", Journal of Finance 2006.
   - Role: Bootstrapping fund returns to separate skill from luck.
   - Links: https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1540-6261.2006.01015.x

15. Taleb - "The Statistical Consequences of Fat Tails (selected chapters)", STEM Academic Press 2020.
   - Role: Why sample statistics are unreliable under fat tails; a caution on small trade samples.
   - Links: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2001.10488

16. Kaminski, Lo - "When Do Stop-Loss Rules Stop Losses?", Journal of Financial Markets 2014.
   - Role: How stops change a strategy's return: they help with momentum and hurt with random walks.
   - Links: https://doi.org/10.1016/j.finmar.2013.07.004

17. Zhu, Zhou - "Technical Analysis: An Asset Allocation Perspective on the Use of Moving Averages", Journal of Financial Economics 2009.
   - Role: Moving averages add value when the market's trend is uncertain.
   - Links: https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jfineco.2009.01.002
